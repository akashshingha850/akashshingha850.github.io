I build robots that see and fly on their own. My work spans **autonomous drones**, **digital twins** and **on-device AI**: GPS-denied flight with PX4 and visual-inertial odometry, simulation-to-reality pipelines in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and real-time perception that runs entirely on embedded hardware such as NVIDIA Jetson.

I'm with the [Biomimetics and Intelligent Systems Group (BISG)](https://www.oulu.fi/en/research-groups/biomimetics-and-intelligent-systems-group) at the University of Oulu, Finland. Current threads:

- **Digital twins for drone fleets.** [bisg_isaac](https://github.com/akashshingha850/bisg_isaac) runs simulated and real PX4/ZED drones through the same ROS 2 containers and code.
- **Active 3D capture.** [Kinetix](https://github.com/akashshingha850/kinetix) plans next-best views so a monocular drone can reconstruct objects and whole rooms in fewer flights.
- **Perception datasets and benchmarks.** LiDAR range-image segmentation of roadside infrastructure ([PoleSight](/publications/)) and UAV scene understanding.
- **Autonomy on the edge.** Onboard YOLO and DeepStream pipelines, from search-and-detect drones ([RAPTOR, UltraHack 2026](/projects/)) to assistive vision for visually impaired users.
