A resource-aware assistive system that helps people with visual disabilities understand their surroundings. Everything runs locally on an NVIDIA Jetson for privacy and low latency.

- Real-time object detection and vision-language scene description.
- GPS positioning with place-name lookup.
- Voice interaction through speech recognition and synthesis.
- MQTT links the components into a distributed system.
