A real-time computer-vision and tracking system for surveillance and reconnaissance.

- **Detection:** YOLO11 / YOLOv8 / YOLOv5 with GPU acceleration through the NVIDIA DeepStream SDK.
- **Video:** RTSP, WebRTC and local sources, with multi-stream processing.
- **Control:** MAVLink integration for drone and gimbal control and telemetry.
- **Ops:** browser dashboard plus live CPU/GPU temperature and load monitoring.
