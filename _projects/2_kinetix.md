_Fewer flights. Smarter views. Better models._

Kinetix plans where a monocular drone should look next so it can reconstruct indoor objects and whole rooms with fewer images. Simulation and flight run on the [bisg_isaac](/projects/1_bisg_isaac/) digital twin (Isaac Sim 6.0 + Pegasus + PX4 + MAVROS).

- The object demo loop and whole-room capture fly end to end on the twin.
- A planned 2-minute tour of the warehouse reconstructs with every keyframe registered and 8 cm camera error.
- Next: online SfM and learned next-best-view scorers.
