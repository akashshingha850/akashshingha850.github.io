  1. CVC
    ROADSIGHT: A Multi-Season UAV Dataset and Benchmark for Road Intersection Detection with Real-Time Embedded Inference
    Akash Shingha Bappy, Siva Ariram, Timo Mäenpää, and Juha Röning
    In Computer Vision Conference (CVC), 2027
    Accepted
    Abs Bib Website

    Detecting road intersections in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) imagery supports aerial mapping, infrastructure monitoring and intelligent transportation. At northern latitudes, snow cover and vegetation change how the same intersection appears from the air, but existing aerial datasets target vehicles or generic road scenes and seldom capture this variation. We introduce ROADSIGHT, to our knowledge the first UAV dataset with intersection-type annotations collected under varied seasonal conditions. It comprises 1,416 RGB images captured in Finland under summer, autumn and winter conditions, with 3,133 bounding boxes for roundabouts, T-intersections and X-intersections. We use site-disjoint training, validation and test splits to prevent spatial leakage. We benchmark 17 YOLO and RT-DETR configurations. RT-DETR-x achieves the highest test mAP50:95 of 0.488, but real-time use also depends on latency, so we select YOLO26n, whose cross-validated accuracy is not distinguishable from that of RT-DETR-l at one-thirteenth of its parameters. Generalization across seasons is the dominant challenge: withholding one period from training reduces accuracy on it by 0.08 to 0.32 mAP50:95, and without winter training images, winter accuracy falls to 0.082. With TensorRT at half precision, YOLO26n processes 86 and 98 frames per second on the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano and Orin NX, respectively. The dataset is publicly available.

  1. IVCNZ
    RangeGen PoleSight: A Benchmark Dataset and Scalable Range-Image Generation Framework for Roadside Pole Detection
    Timo Mäenpää*, Akash Bappy*, Siva Ariram, Durga Prasad Bavirisetti, and Antti Tikanmäki
    In International Conference on Image and Vision Computing New Zealand (IVCNZ). *Equal contribution , 2026
    Abs Bib Website

    Roadside pole-like infrastructure supports asset inventory, high-definition mapping, and autonomous-vehicle localization, yet Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) benchmarks with fine-grained pole categories remain scarce. This paper presents PoleSight, a range-image instance-segmentation dataset, and RangeGen, a scalable framework for generating annotated range views from large-scale mobile laser scanning point clouds. RangeGen projects dense point clouds from virtual sensor poses, transfers manually verified two-dimensional annotations back to their source three-dimensional points, and reprojects the labeled cloud to obtain additional annotated frames without repeated manual labeling. PoleSight provides 1,088 range images with return intensity and 6,121 polygon instances of light, fence, traffic, and gantry-sign poles. Fifteen segmentation baselines, five variants each from three YOLO families, reach a best box mean average precision (mAP) of 29.21 and a best mask value of 11.93 across standard COCO thresholds on a seen-location split, in which test frames usually show poles that are also present in training. That gap identifies precise mask recovery, rather than localization, as the open challenge on this benchmark.

  2. Sci. Rep.
    SENSEYE: A Resource-Aware Visionary Framework for Assisting Individuals with Visual Disabilities
    Akash Shingha Bappy, Tapio Seppänen, and Md Ziaul Hoque
    Scientific Reports, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML Code

    Despite significant recent advances, visual aid systems are still limited by the use of conventional computer vision algorithms, constrained sensor capabilities, high power consumption, and reliance on cloud-based processing, which introduce latency and privacy risks. This paper introduces SENSEYE, a resource-aware visionary framework that employs edge computing with a secure and competent communication mechanism. The proposed architecture integrates IoT Edge and virtual decentralized services in a portable system that is small, cost-effective, and power-efficient. SENSEYE integrates real-time object detection, scene comprehension, and GPS-based navigation into a portable, low-latency device, leveraging optimized lightweight AI models such as SSD-MobileNetV2 and VILA1.5-3b to provide accurate environmental awareness and seamless auditory feedback. The system also enables secure remote assistance via video streaming and real-time GPS location sharing. The evaluations confirm superior accuracy, power efficiency, and responsiveness compared to traditional sensor-based or cloud-reliant systems.
